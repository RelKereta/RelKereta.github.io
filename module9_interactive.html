Decarbonising the Grid: Electricity Transition (1985–2023)

Tracking the structural shift from fossil baseloads to renewable power

Timeline Range: 1985 to 2023
Accessibility Summary (Alt-Text): Interactive dashboard detailing electricity production across primary fuel sources from 1985 to 2023. The main plotting canvas is locked to prevent accidental dragging. A dedicated timeline scrubber synchronously controls both the multi-fuel Combined Overview and the 9-panel Faceted Breakdown.

Intentional Interactivity Features:
  • Canvas Drag Lock (dragmode=False): Disables canvas dragging so user interactions remain focused exclusively on intentional timeline filtering and hover inspection.
  • Cross-View Synchronized Timeline: Unified timeline slider dynamically updates the visible year boundaries across both the combined view and all 9 faceted subplots simultaneously.
  • Faceted Small Multiples: Resolves scale compression by separating low-magnitude clean fuels from legacy coal baselines.
  • Country Dropdown & Metric Switcher: Instantly compares national portfolios and switches between physical generation volume (TWh) and relative grid proportion (% Share).

Data Source: Our World in Data (Ember & Energy Institute). Filtered to verified records (1985–2023).
